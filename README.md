This is my first game project. I am learning GODOT as I program it, I have a degree in Computer Science and Computer Information Technologies
This is primarily for fun, but if the game comes together and my friends find it fun, I may try to do something with it

It originally started off as an idea for a Card Collecting/Deck Building game, almost as a joke; and I began building it in Python, but I quickly decided that was untenable
I decided I would pivot and push myself to use a real game engine and found that GODOT was recommended for Card Games (and that even Megacrit used it)

so I instead decided that I would use the world/lore/story from my writing and D&D campaigns to develop a Rogue-lite, like Roguebook or Slay the Spire, originally still thinking it would be a deck-builder type game
But as I began developing it in GODOT (first just a character, a dummy enemy, and combat) I quickly realized I was doing a lot of stuff that was more RPG like — the Basic attack card was always returning to hand
the energy system was similar to something like Expedition 33, and wanting to embrace some stats/rpg elements (leveling up and character building — not just deck building), I instead embraced an initiative based system
So now you still have to learn abilities and they are randomized when you pick that option, but you can only equip a total of 6, with one of them locked (the character's unique/signature ability)

if anyone even looks at this, it's a bit of a mess due to learning the language while building it and expanding from just a combat scene/script to much, much more over the course of about a month; feel free to reach out
if you find it interesting or want to know more about my thoughts or processes or even are interested in possibly collaborating.
